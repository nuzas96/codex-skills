# Legacy Evidence Lessons

Load when preserving the user's original evidence-first operating discipline during difficult or multi-turn solves.

- Keep at most five ranked hypotheses, each with supporting evidence, cheapest discriminating test, and positive/negative signal.
- Confirm primitives independently before composing a chain; label claims confirmed, likely, untested, or disproved.
- Treat errors, response differences, crashes, and environment drift as evidence.
- Request only the smallest one to three focused outputs needed to unblock progress.
- Accept a flag only with exact provenance, deterministic reproduction, format consistency, hint consistency, and remote confirmation when local placeholders exist.
- Preserve failed paths and do not retry without new evidence.
