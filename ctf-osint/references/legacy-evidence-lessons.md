# Legacy Evidence Lessons

Load for multilingual entity matching, chained OSINT challenges, Discord/community evidence, or infrastructure pivots.

- Prefer rare anchors such as phone numbers, uncommon phrases, usernames, exact image text, and page titles over generic imagery.
- Copy exact text before translating; search accented and normalized local-language variants while preserving proper nouns.
- Reuse confirmed entities across sequential challenges and retain the relationship between earlier and later clues.
- Treat Discord flags, deleted-message fragments, bot output, and community-page strings as candidates until chronology and challenge provenance are proven.
- Record why a result is the same entity, not merely a matching name.
- Certificate logs and broad DNS results may identify infrastructure without revealing an origin; record negative searches and pivot to repository identity/history or exact-title indexes only when justified.
- Do not normalize punctuation, case, diacritics, or symbols unless the flag rules explicitly require it.
